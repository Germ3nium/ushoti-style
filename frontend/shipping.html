Shipping and Delivery Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

We deliver nationwide across South Africa through trusted door-to-door courier services (such as The Courier Guy and Aramex) to physical street addresses, and through PAXI to PAXI collection points.

1. Fulfilment & Processing Times

Standard Delivery: Please allow about 2 weeks from the day your payment clears until your parcel arrives. This includes sourcing, verifying and packing your order, as well as courier or PAXI transit time.

Express Delivery: If you need your order sooner, you can choose Express courier delivery at checkout where it is available. The price and estimated delivery time are shown before you pay.

Orders placed on weekends or public holidays are processed from the following business day.

3. Real-Time Tracking

Once the courier collects your parcel:

A shipping confirmation email and SMS containing your live tracking number and tracking link will be sent automatically.

Courier deliveries take place during standard business hours (08:00 to 17:00, Monday to Friday). A physical signature is required upon delivery.

PAXI orders are collected from the PAXI point you choose at checkout. Please enter a valid cell phone number so that you can be notified when your parcel is ready for collection.

4. Failed Deliveries & Address Corrections

Please ensure that all delivery details, unit/apartment numbers, PAXI point details, and contact cell phone numbers are entered accurately during checkout. Ushoti Style is not liable for delivery delays caused by incomplete address submissions. If a parcel is returned to sender due to unreachable consignees, re-delivery fees may apply.
