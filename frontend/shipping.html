Shipping and Delivery Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

We deliver nationwide to physical street addresses across South Africa through trusted door-to-door courier services (such as The Courier Guy and Aramex).

1. Fulfilment & Processing Times

Order Verification & Packing: Orders are verified and packaged within 24 to 48 hours (business days, Monday to Friday) after payment has successfully cleared.

Orders placed on weekends or public holidays are prepared on the following business day.

2. Delivery Rates & Turnaround Times

3. Real-Time Tracking

Once the courier collects your parcel:

A shipping confirmation email and SMS containing your live tracking number and tracking link will be sent automatically.

Deliveries take place during standard business hours (08:00 to 17:00, Monday to Friday). A physical signature is required upon delivery.

4. Failed Deliveries & Address Corrections

Please ensure that all delivery details, unit/apartment numbers, and contact cell phone numbers are entered accurately during checkout. Ushoti Style is not liable for delivery delays caused by incomplete address submissions. If a parcel is returned to sender due to unreachable consignees, re-delivery fees may apply.
